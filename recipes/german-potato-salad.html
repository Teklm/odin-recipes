Authentic German Potato Salad

Description

This warm German potato salad features tender potatoes tossed in a flavourful and tangy bacon vinaigrette. Pairs perfectly with grilled sausages or schnitzel. It is usually served warm instead of cold, but some people prefer to serve at room temperature.

Prep Time: 15 mins

Cook Time: 25 mins

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 3 cups diced peeled potatoes
  • 4 slices bacon
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp water
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp white sugar, or more to taste
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/8 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Gather ingredients. Place potatoes in a large pot and add water to cover by at least 1 to 2 inches. Cook until potatoes are easily pierced with a fork, about 10 minutes. Drain, set aside to cool.
  2. While potatoes are cooking, place bacon in large, deep skillet over medium-high heat. Fry until browned and crisp, 10-12 minutes, turning as needed. Transfer bacon to a paper-towel lined plate and crumble when cool enough to handle. Leave bacon grease in the skillet.
  3. Cook onion in the bacon grease over medium heat until browned, 6-8 minutes. Add vinegar, sugar, water, salt, and pepper to the pan and bring to a boil.
  4. Once boiling, add potatoes and 1/2 of crumbled bacon. Cook until heated through, 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Transfer warm potato salad to serving dish and sprinkle remaining crumbled bacon over top. Garnish with parsley and serve warm.
