Veggie Pot Pie

Description

A vegetarian pot pie, rich with flavour, made with potatoes, mushrooms, cauliflower, green beans, and carrots. Serves 6.

Prep Time = 30 mins

Cook Time: 1 hour

Servings: 6

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 large carrots, diced
  • 2 potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 2 stalks celery, sliced 1/4 inch wide
  • 2 cups cauliflower forets
  • 1 cup fresh green beans, trimmed and snapped into 1/2 inch pieces
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 (14.1 ounce) package double-crust pie pastry, thawed

Steps

  1. Gather ingredients. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C).
  2. Heat oil in large skillet or saucepan. Add mushrooms, onions, and garlic; cook for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
  3. Stir in carrots, potatoes, and celery, then stir in cauliflower and green beans; add vegetable broth and bring to boil.
  4. Reduce heat and simmer until vegetables are just tender, about 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Mix water, cornstarch, and soy sauce in a small bowl until cornstarch is completely dissolved; stir into vegetables and cook until sauce thickens, about 3 minutes.
  6. Roll out 1/2 of dough to line an 11x7 inch baking dish. Pour vegetable filling into the pastry-lined dish. Roll out remaining dough; arrange over filling, then seal and flute the edges.
  7. Bake in preheated oven until pie is golden brown on top, about 30 minutes.
